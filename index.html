About this study

Where the campus's event knowledge actually lives

Every college fest, hackathon, and workshop runs on knowledge that rarely gets written down — it lives in one team's group chat and disappears when they graduate. I interviewed ten organizers across six event types about how they planned, what broke, and what they wish someone had told them, then looked for the pattern underneath all ten answers.
